04 — About

Academic Profile

Research at the intersection of mechanical engineering, thermodynamics and sustainable energy.

Selçuk Selimli is an Associate Professor in the field of Mechanical Engineering, with research activities concentrated on energy and renewable energy systems.

His research includes photovoltaic technologies, photovoltaic-thermal systems, energy and exergy analysis, solar tracking, PV cooling technologies, thermodynamics and sustainable energy conversion.

His work combines experimental investigation, thermodynamic analysis and performance evaluation to improve the efficiency and sustainability of energy systems.
