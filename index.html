Research

Research Areas

My research focuses on the development, thermodynamic assessment and optimization of sustainable energy systems, with particular emphasis on solar energy, photovoltaic technologies, heat transfer and advanced thermofluids.

01

Solar Energy & Photovoltaic Systems

Photovoltaic systems, PV/T technologies, solar tracking, partial shading, dust effects and solar energy harvesting.

02

Energy & Exergy Analysis

Energy efficiency, exergy efficiency, exergy destruction, sustainability and thermodynamic performance assessment.

03

PV Thermal Management

PV cooling, fin structures, spray cooling, phase-change materials and nanofluid-based thermal management.

04

Heat Transfer Enhancement

Heat exchangers, heat pipes, twisted tapes, corrugated tubes and thermo-hydraulic performance enhancement.

05

CFD, MHD & Thermofluids

Computational fluid dynamics, numerical modeling, magnetohydrodynamics and advanced thermofluid systems.

06

Sustainable Energy Systems

Renewable energy technologies, solar thermal systems, waste heat recovery and sustainable energy applications.

Academic Profile

I am an Associate Professor of Mechanical Engineering at Karabük University, Türkiye. My research interests encompass renewable energy systems, solar energy, photovoltaic and photovoltaic-thermal technologies, energy and exergy analysis, heat transfer enhancement, computational fluid dynamics and advanced thermofluids.
